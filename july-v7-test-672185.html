Personal setup

Build your plan

Add your details and the app suggests a starting point for calories and protein. Everything stays editable, adjust to your body and routine.

Step 1 · About you

Your session time sets the finish point marked inside every workout, so the plan fits your real day. Your goal shapes calories and workout focus, more in the Guide tab.

Step 2 · Your targets

A suggested starting point, not a rule. Snacks, coffees and life outside the plan are fine, the planner shows what each day leaves you to play with.

Step 3 · Your meals

Fewer recipes means easier meal prep, pick 2 or 3 of each and cook them in one go for the week. Snacks work the same way, choose 3 snacks and the app rotates them across every day, batch-make them once and they cover the whole week. You can still swap any day in the planner.

Step 4 · Your training
Days free to train
Include / exclude specific workouts · optional

Leave this alone and the app rotates this month's workouts for you. Only use it if you want to force a favourite in, or keep one out.

Planner built

Your weekly planner

How to use your week. Tap adjust on any meal to resize the portion (−10% / +10%), swap an ingredient or read the recipe. Skip means you are not having that meal today. Choose "Custom / my own" in any dropdown to log something outside the plan. Days do not need to land exactly on target, close is perfect, and balance will gently scale a day for you.
Meal bank

Golden Hour recipes

Every recipe from this month's plan. Open any meal to scale servings, see the method and use relevant swaps. Tap the heart to save a favourite, swapped versions save too.

Workout bank

8 Golden Hour workouts

Four gym and four home sessions. Each one has three finish points, your session time in the planner sets which is highlighted for you.

Shopping list
