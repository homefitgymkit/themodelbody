This Month

The theme of the month

The Reset

September is the return to routine. Not an aggressive cut, not making up for summer. This month rebuilds the simple habits that make you feel strong and organised again: four structured strength sessions, a home mirror for every one of them, and a plan you can actually repeat.

This month's focus

Train with intent and write it down. Two lower-body days built on hip thrusts and Romanian deadlifts, a push day and a V-taper pull day, plus four completely refreshed ten-minute ab blocks.

What to expect

Four training days, each with a matching home version, so no gym access never becomes a missed week. Every session finishes with a ten-minute core block and a finisher for those who want it.

How this training supports your goal

The same four sessions repeated all month, so you can track what you lift. Week 1 sets the baseline. Beat it by Week 4 on one lower-body and one upper-body lift and the month has done its job.

Kassy's training note

Write your weights and reps down this month. I want Week 4 to have something to beat. Your home workout counts exactly the same as the gym version when that is what fits your day. Consistency is the goal; the equipment is just the tool.

Challenge System

Monthly challenges

One shared challenge across training and meals, plus four weekly missions. The point is showing up, not being perfect.

The Reset · 7-Day Challenge
Seven days to get the routine back

One small habit a day for seven days. The same seven days appear in your Meal Plan, so you only have to run this once.

Day 1
Plan your four training slots and choose your meals for the next three days
Day 2
Protein at every main meal plus a 20-minute walk
Day 3
Track your weights and reps in your workout and hit 2 litres of water
Day 4
Complete one 10-minute ab workout and prep tomorrow's breakfast or snack
Day 5
Add fruit or veg to three meals and hit your step goal
Day 6
Complete 10 minutes of mobility or recovery and make one high-protein sweet
Day 7
Review your week: one win, one thing to improve, and book next week's sessions
Four weekly Reset Missions — one goal per week, each building on the last
Week 1 · Routine Reset

Choose your Gym, Home or Hybrid schedule, complete at least 3 sessions, log your starting weights and prep two grab-and-go food options.

Week 2 · Strength Reset

Complete the planned week and improve at least two tracked lifts by one rep, a small load increase or cleaner control.

Week 3 · Core + Consistency

Complete all four 10-minute ab blocks across the week, keep the rest day, and use the home version instead of skipping if plans change.

Week 4 · Finish Stronger

Beat Week 1 on one lower-body lift and one upper-body lift, choose your four favourite meal repeats and write down three habits to carry into October.

Optional: the 30-Day Reset Streak

Want a daily version? Score one Reset point for every day you tick all five: move as planned (workout, recovery session or a 30-minute intentional walk), protein at every main meal, 2 litres of water, your daily step target, and a 10-minute reset for tomorrow (prep food, stretch, plan your session or set up your morning).

Train Smarter Each Week

Progression, swaps & form

How the month builds, how to swap any move when the equipment is taken, and the cues that keep your form clean.

Week 1

Learn the workouts, choose clean starting loads and record your reps. This is your baseline.

Week 2

Add reps, improve control or trim unnecessary rest. Nothing dramatic.

Week 3

Add a small amount of load, range, band tension or a harder variation.

Week 4

Push intensity without losing form. Beat Week 1 on one lower-body and one upper-body lift.

The rule

Reps in reserve: finish main lifts with 1–2 clean reps in the tank. Add weight when you hit the top of the rep range across all sets with clean form. Overload is weight, reps, sets, band tension, range, tempo or control. One per week is enough.

Write it down

The one non-negotiable this month. Log weight and reps on every main lift. Week 4 needs something to beat.

Home counts the same

Missing the gym is not missing the week. The home mirror hits the same muscles in the same order.

When to rest more

If form breaks down or sleep is poor, take the extra rest day. Recovery is when the work pays off.

Glutes
Swaps

Barbell hip thrust→sofa DB hip thrust / glute bridge / banded bridge
Lateral high-box step-up→lower step / bodyweight lateral step-up
Diagonal cable kickback→banded or ankle-weight kickback
Abduction machine→seated band abduction / side-lying abduction

Form

Push through the working heel, squeeze for two seconds and control the lower. Ribs down. If you cannot feel the glute, slow the rep before adding load.

Hamstrings
Swaps

Barbell RDL→DB RDL / single-leg RDL
Smith Bulgarian split squat→rear-foot-elevated DB split squat
Lying leg curl→slider or towel hamstring curl
45° back extension→single-leg glute bridge / hip hinge with band

Form

Three-second lower on every RDL. Soft knees, long spine, lats tight, bar close. Move through the hips, and stop where the hamstrings stop.

Push + Shoulders
Swaps

Seated DB shoulder press→half-kneeling single-arm press / band press
Machine lateral raise→DB lateral raise / bottle raise
Cable Y-raise→incline Y-raise / band Y-raise
Single-arm cable tricep extension→cross-body DB extension / band pushdown

Form

Lead with the elbows on raises and keep the traps quiet. Ribs down overhead. Lock the upper arm on triceps work so only the elbow moves.

Back + Core
Swaps

Pull-ups→assisted pull-up / door-anchored band pulldown
Chest-supported row→DB wide row / band row
One-arm cable lat row→one-arm DB row
Face pull→band face pull / pull-apart

Form

Drive the elbows toward the hips, chest proud, no shrugging. On core work keep the ribs tucked. If the lower back arches on leg work, shorten the lever.
