How to use

Your July guide inside the app

Fat Loss

Prioritise consistency, 3–4 workouts, steps and simple repeated meals. Use the no-track plate guide.

Balanced Toning

The main route. Train 4 days, repeat meals where useful, and focus on progressive overload.

Glute Growth

Prioritise glutes A/B, eat enough protein and avoid turning every day into cardio.

No-track plate

½ plate fruit/veg, ¼ protein, ¼ carbs, add a thumb of fats if needed. Build around protein first.

Swaps guide

Use dairy-free yoghurt, tofu/beans for vegan, low-sodium soy, rice/potatoes instead of higher-bloat swaps.

Disclaimer

This is general fitness and nutrition guidance, not medical advice. Adjust to your body and seek professional support where needed.

Progression, swaps and form

Progress by adding a rep, slowing tempo or adding a little weight. Keep glutes controlled, ribs down and avoid rushing reps. If equipment is missing, use the home equipment section in Train.
